# Championship Manager / Football Manager — Overview

Sports Interactive's management series, running since 1992: Championship Manager until the 2004 split with publisher Eidos, Football Manager since. This folder follows the Sports Interactive line and ignores the later Eidos-made Championship Manager games.

## What It Is

A management sim, not a football game you play. You pick the squad and the tactics; the match runs itself and you influence it from the touchline.

- **A deep player model** — dozens of attributes per player, visible and hidden, on top of a hidden ability budget. [Player Attributes](player-attributes.md)
- **Careers measured in decades** — players develop, decline, come through a youth intake and retire, and the squad you inherit is not the one you finish with
- **A whole world simulated** — every league's fixtures play out each season, whether or not anyone watches
- **Summary layers over the detail** — star ratings, role suitability and traits exist so the depth stays readable

## Why It's Here

SWOS is thin in exactly the places FM is deep, and it's thin partly because of what a mid-90s machine could hold. Where we want more than SWOS has, FM has decades of a worked answer to read — and a visible cost to weigh, because every attribute it adds makes a decision slower.

Which of those we'd actually take is a question for [Ideas](../../project/ideas.md), not this folder: this is what FM does, Ideas is what we might want.

It's not a reference for match feel or input. Its match engine is something you watch and influence, not something you control.
