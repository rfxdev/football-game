# Championship Manager / Football Manager — Overview

Sports Interactive's management series, running since 1992: Championship Manager until the 2004 split with publisher Eidos, Football Manager since. This folder follows the Sports Interactive line and ignores the later Eidos-made Championship Manager games. Kept split by concern, as [SWOS](../swos/overview.md) is: [Player Attributes](player-attributes.md) so far.

## Why It's a Reference

Management-sim depth is out of scope, so FM isn't a model to copy. It's the far end of a scale that SWOS sits at the other end of, and it's useful where SWOS leaves an open question:

- **Ability that changes over a career.** SWOS skills are [fixed for life](../swos/overview.md#seven-skills-fixed-for-life). FM has had players develop and decline for decades, which shows what that needs underneath and what it costs the player to read.
- **What depth costs.** Every attribute FM adds makes a decision slower. That tells us where to stop, even if we stop long before FM does.
- **Simulating matches nobody watches.** FM plays out a whole world's fixtures every season, which is relevant to [CPU vs CPU Simulation](../../docs/design/tournament-and-career-mode/cpu-vs-cpu-simulation.md).

It's not a reference for match feel or input. Its match engine is something you watch and influence, not something you control.
