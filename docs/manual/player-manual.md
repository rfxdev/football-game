# Player Manual

## Controls

One direction and one button. That's the whole game.

| | Pad | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Move** | Left stick or d-pad | Arrow keys |
| **The button** | A | Space |

## Running With the Ball

Run onto the ball to take it. You're a little slower with it, and it runs a touch ahead of you as you go — keep running and you catch it again.

Turn while it's at your feet and it turns with you. Turn while it's running ahead and you leave it behind. A good dribbler keeps it closer, so has more time to turn.

Let go of the stick and you stop. The ball rolls on.
