# Ideas

Where the game could go — before any of it has been checked against anything. No commitment, no promise to a player, no acceptance criteria. Just notes worth not losing.

This page is the outline: a line per area. The ideas themselves are in that area's file in [`ideas/`](ideas/). Add an idea to the file it belongs in whenever one occurs; one that fits no area gets a new file and a line here. Say what it gains, not what it would change — working that out, and building the case, is what the gate is for.

## After the SWOS Match Clone

The planned phases end at a match clone. Past that, two stages: reach parity with SWOS's hybrid loop — the match plus the manager game around it — then go beyond it.

### Stage 1 — Hybrid Parity

What SWOS has around the match that the roadmap doesn't build yet.

- [Front End](ideas/front-end.md) — pause, options, replays, and the menus past a friendly
- [Controls](ideas/controls.md) — two-player couch co-op, and the keyboard
- [Tactics Beyond Formations](ideas/tactics-beyond-formations.md) — fit advice, a training match, a shape preview, instructions
- [Career](ideas/career.md) — SWOS-weight, with static attributes: season, squad upkeep, transfers, finances, and international management

### Stage 2 — Beyond SWOS

Tracks, mostly independent of each other.

- [Match Play](ideas/match-play.md) — attacking variety without a second button
- [Player Depth](ideas/player-depth.md) — more attributes, attributes that drive AI and fixing speed dominance, then form and progression
- [Management](ideas/management.md) — career roles past player-manager, and depth towards FM
- [Presentation](ideas/presentation.md) — high-resolution 2D or 3D, plus zoom, pitch wear and a UI overhaul
- [World](ideas/world.md) — community packs

## Graduating an Idea

When one is next, run it through [the gate](../ways-of-working/spec-chain.md#the-gate).

- Passed → onto the [Roadmap](roadmap.md), into a phase, and out of its area's file
- Declined → `docs/decisions/`, with why
