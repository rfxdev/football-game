# 0.3 Ball Physics Test Suite

**Status:** Outline

A GdUnit4 suite over the fake-Z ball, from day one. Correctness, not feel — no thresholds on anything still being tuned.

**Notes:**

- **This is the cheapest test coverage the project will ever get, and everything inherits what it fails to catch.** Height is a plain float with no rendering involved, so it tests at close to zero cost, while every system built above it assumes the arithmetic underneath is right. Rationale for what to automate and why in [Automation Testing](../../ways-of-working/automation-testing.md)
- **From day one, not after the first milestone.** Retrofitting it means writing tests against behaviour you've already started trusting

**Testing:**

- Height integrates under gravity and comes to rest rather than jittering
- A bounce loses and keeps exactly the normal pitch's fractions of ground and vertical speed, per [Pitch Conditions](../../docs/design/match-engine/ball-physics-and-aerial-simulation.md#pitch-conditions)
- Ball and shadow drawn positions as functions of height
- The goal predicate — line crossed **and** under the bar — tested at its corners
- Ground speed falls at the air rate at every height above 0, per [Fake Height](../../docs/design/match-engine/ball-physics-and-aerial-simulation.md#fake-height)

The goal predicate gets corner cases specifically because it is two terms ANDed together, and that is where the bugs live.
