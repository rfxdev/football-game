# 6.2 Team Management

**Status:** Outline

Choosing who plays and how, before a match and during one: pick a formation from [4.2](4.2-extra-formations.md)'s set, place players from [6.1 Squads](6.1-squads.md) into its grid cells, and change formation or make substitutions mid-match. Formations only — no team or player instructions, and no fit advice.

**Notes:**

- **The first deliberate departure from the reference.** SWOS's per-zone tactics editor and its Manager's Bench, in [Tactics and Team Selection](../../sources/swos/tactics-and-team-selection.md) and [Match Mechanics](../../sources/swos/match-mechanics.md), are what this replaces rather than clones. The UI is original design, in the same direction as team management generally
- **Flow is the thing it is judged on.** Changing formation or bringing someone on mid-match shouldn't cost the thread of the match — SWOS's editor is the failure mode it exists to avoid
- **Built for a controller.** Moving a player cell by cell with the d-pad, not placing them freely with a stick
- **The screen edits a layout, and a layout is data** — the same format CPU teams and [4.2](4.2-extra-formations.md)'s set use. Whether a player can save their own layouts is to decide
- **When changes are allowed is to decide.** SWOS let formation changes happen whenever the ball was dead, and substitutions only when it was out of play
- **Out-of-position effects land here at the earliest.** [6.1 Squads](6.1-squads.md) records each player's natural position, but this is the first time a player can be put in a cell that isn't theirs. SWOS changes their effective skills while they're there ([Positional Effects](../../sources/swos/player-ratings.md#positional-effects)); whether to clone that or replace it is still open
- **Substitutions are the first way off for an injured player.** Since [5.4 Cards and Injuries](5.4-cards-and-injuries.md) they have played on with a stat malus

**Testing:** layout validity as a table — one player per cell, ten outfield, keeper fixed — for anything the screen can produce. A mid-match formation change moves every player's anchor on the next update, with no behaviour code involved.
