# 3.2 Shape and Home Zones

**Status:** Outline

The formation grid: a 2×2 grid holding a 2-2 in front of the keeper, moving as one block to follow the ball, with each player returning to their cell when not engaged. This is the change that turns [3.1](3.1-dumb-five-a-side.md)'s chaos into something recognisable as football. One layout only. Both columns are edge columns, so the wide-column rules apply from the start.

**Asking:** Did the blob resolve into something that reads as football? Does play have flow? Are goals happening at a reasonable rate? Does the shape follow the ball without lurching when it moves fast?

**Session shape:** full scenarios, **with a friend if at all possible** — two perspectives catch far more than one, and a second player exposes the things you've stopped noticing

**Notes:**

- **This is the milestone that "blobbing" is the named failure mode *for*.** All outfield players converging on the ball reads as chaos rather than football. It's a shape problem more than an AI-intelligence problem, with a specific mechanism behind it — separation steering and role-constrained targets, per [Team AI and Decision Making](../../docs/design/match-engine/team-ai-and-decision-making.md) and [Formation and Shape System](../../docs/design/match-engine/formation-and-shape-system.md). If it survives this milestone, those weights are the first place to look rather than the decision logic
- **The opposite failure mode arrives here too: bumper cars.** Players glued to their cells look as wrong as players chasing the ball, just differently wrong. Watch for whether anyone ever *breaks* shape to press or support, and whether they snap back afterwards. "Nobody left their cell" is a finding worth logging as loudly as blobbing
- **Grid dimensions are data from the start.** [4.1](4.1-eleven-a-side.md) moves to a 5×5 grid and will need code for what the extra cells add, but hard-wiring 2×2 here turns that into the rewrite 4.1 warns about. Block size and anchors are fractions of the pitch for a similar reason — the pitch changes size at 4.1
- **The possession nudge is built here and judged here.** Play with it at zero and with it on. If it adds nothing visible, it stays at zero
- Goal rate is the most useful single number here, and one of the few subjective questions with an objective proxy — but it isn't sampleable in bulk until the headless harness at [5.5](5.5-clock-halves-and-full-time.md)

**Testing:** anchors as a pure function — layout, ball position and possession nudge in, every player's anchor out — table-tested against the always-true criteria in [Formation and Shape System](../../docs/design/match-engine/formation-and-shape-system.md#acceptance-criteria), with the ball in either box and on both touchlines. Shape recovery — park the ball, run on, assert every uninvolved player is back within radius of their cell's anchor for that ball position.

**360 Testing:** now that home zones exist, [3.1](3.1-dumb-five-a-side.md)'s blobbing observation is assertable for the first time — team spread, or mean distance from each player to their cell's anchor, as a numeric *floor* rather than a look-and-judge call.
