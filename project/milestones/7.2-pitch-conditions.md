# 7.2 Pitch Conditions

**Status:** Outline

The other six surfaces around normal: each changes the ball's ground friction and bounce, per [Ball Physics](../../docs/design/match-engine/ball-physics-and-aerial-simulation.md#pitch-conditions), and the colour of the grass, per [Pitch and Environment](../../docs/design/match-engine/pitch-and-environment.md#pitch-conditions). No player is affected, and nothing is drawn on the pitch — no weather, no wear.

**Notes:**

- **Picked on [7.1 Team Select](7.1-team-select.md)'s setup screen**, alongside random
- **The other six add to normal's ground friction.** If a surface rolls wrong, check normal's friction before the table
- **Conditions that touch the players would be original design.** The reference's never do, so anything like rain slowing a run goes through [the gate](../../ways-of-working/spec-chain.md#the-gate) first

**Sprites:** a grass palette per condition.
