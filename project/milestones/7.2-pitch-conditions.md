# 7.2 Pitch Conditions

**Status:** Outline

The other six surfaces around normal, per [Pitch Conditions](../../docs/design/match-engine/pitch-and-environment.md#pitch-conditions): each changes the ball's ground friction and bounce, and the colour of the grass. No player is affected, and nothing is drawn on the pitch — no weather, no wear.

**Notes:**

- **Picked on [7.1 Team Select](7.1-team-select.md)'s setup screen**, alongside random
- **Ground friction on the other six is a multiple of normal's.** If a surface rolls wrong, check normal's friction before the table
- **Conditions that touch the players would be original design.** The reference's never do, so anything like rain slowing a run goes through [the gate](../../ways-of-working/spec-chain.md#the-gate) first

**Sprites:** a grass palette per condition.
