# 5.2 Out of Play

**Status:** Outline

Throw-ins, corners and goal kicks. Boundary detection already exists from [0.2 Sandbox Scene](0.2-sandbox-scene.md) — what this milestone adds is the restart that follows instead of a scenario reset.

**Notes:**

- **Correctness-shaped, not feel-shaped.** By this point feel is established and what remains is edge cases: where exactly the ball left play, who touched it last, which restart that implies. Best tested adversarially — put the ball out in awkward places on purpose — rather than with a bespoke playtest protocol
- **Routinely underestimated.** The transitions are fiddly and the edge cases are numerous, and none of it is visible in a feature list
- No new input — set-piece kicks use [1.2 Kicking](1.2-kicking.md)'s scheme
- The scenario reset that carried Phases 0–4 retires here, or stays on as a test affordance

**Sprites:** the throw-in. Corners and goal kicks reuse [1.2](1.2-kicking.md)'s kick.

**Testing:** where-the-ball-left-play mapped to the correct restart and position; set pieces fuzzed adversarially with the ball ejected at randomised, awkward positions. A soft-lock detector — never in a restart state longer than N ticks — joins the invariants.
