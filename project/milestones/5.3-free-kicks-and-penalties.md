# 5.3 Free Kicks and Penalties

**Status:** Outline

The restart for fouls detected back at [2.3 Tackling](2.3-tackling.md): a free kick, or a penalty inside the box.

**Notes:**

- **The foul has been detected since [2.3](2.3-tackling.md); only the ceremony is new.** That split — detection with the mechanic, ceremony with the match — is what kept the whole restart system out of Phase 2
- A foul always stops play and always produces a free kick or penalty, decided independently of whether it also draws a card. Keeping those two decisions separate matches the reference and stops [5.4 Cards and Injuries](5.4-cards-and-injuries.md) from becoming a precondition for this one
- Penalties use [1.2 Kicking](1.2-kicking.md)'s scheme

**Attributes:** every penalty is a shot from inside the box, so Finishing's multiplier from [2.1](2.1-keeper-drill.md) applies to all of them.
