# 3.1 Dumb Five a Side

**Status:** Outline

5-a-side with every AI player holding zero tactical intelligence: run at the ball when it's near, kick it vaguely goalward. Deliberately stupid, and the point is that it exposes possession and ball contention all at once while the AI is still too simple to be the suspect.

**Asking:** When several players converge, who ends up with the ball, and is that resolution legible? Does possession change hands cleanly?

**Session shape:** short scenarios, watching more than playing — much of this milestone is judged by eye from a distance rather than through the controller

**Notes:**

- **It will blob, and that is the expected result, not a defect.** Every player runs at the ball because you told them to. The value of this session is everything the blob *reveals*, not the blob itself
- **Ball contention is the thing to watch hardest.** Log *which* resolutions felt unjust and why — arrival order? momentum? nothing visible? The player should be able to tell why they lost the ball. The mechanism is in [On-the-Ball Mechanics](../../docs/design/match-engine/on-the-ball-mechanics.md)
- Blobbing is a numeric property, not only a look, but there is nothing here yet to measure it against — no home zones to compare a player's position to. That arrives at [3.2 Shape and Home Zones](3.2-shape-and-home-zones.md), whose 360 Testing field picks this up
