# 3.1 Dumb Five a Side

**Status:** Outline

5-a-side with every AI player holding zero tactical intelligence: run at the ball when it's near, kick it vaguely goalward. Deliberately stupid, and the point is that it exposes possession, player-vs-player collision and ball contention all at once while the AI is still too simple to be the suspect.

**Asking:** When several players converge, who ends up with the ball, and is that resolution legible? Do bodies collide sensibly? Does possession change hands cleanly?

**Session shape:** short scenarios, watching more than playing — much of this milestone is judged by eye from a distance rather than through the controller

**Notes:**

- **It will blob, and that is the expected result, not a defect.** Every player runs at the ball because you told them to. The value of this session is everything the blob *reveals*, not the blob itself
- **Ball contention is the thing to watch hardest.** Two or three players inside reach radius simultaneously is the genuinely difficult case, and it will feel wrong for a long time before it feels right. Log *which* resolutions felt unjust and why — arrival order? momentum? nothing visible? — per [Ball Interaction System](../../docs/design/match-engine/ball-interaction-system.md)
- **Legibility is the acceptance criterion, not correctness.** The player should be able to tell *why* they lost the ball
- Blobbing is a numeric property, not only a look, but there is nothing here yet to measure it against — no home zones to compare a player's position to. That arrives at [3.2 Shape and Home Zones](3.2-shape-and-home-zones.md), whose 360 Testing field picks this up
