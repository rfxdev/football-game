# 5.1 Kick-off

**Status:** Outline

The first real restart: both teams in formation positions, ball on the centre spot, play resumes on the kick. Every other restart is a variation on the machinery this one establishes.

**Notes:**

- **This is where "where do the players stand?" finally has an answer**, which is why the whole of Phase 5 waited for [4.2 Extra Formations](4.2-extra-formations.md). Kick-off positions are a function of formation, and building them before the formation set existed would have meant building them twice
- Kick-off is also the simplest restart — known positions, no question of where the ball left play or who touched it last — so it is the right one to establish the state machine on before the awkward cases
- See [Match State Machine](../../docs/design/match-engine/match-state-machine.md)

**Sprites:** goal celebrations. Goals have been scored since [2.1](2.1-keeper-drill.md), but the gap between a goal and the kick-off it leads to only exists now.

**Testing:** the transition table tested as a table — legal transitions in, illegal ones rejected.
