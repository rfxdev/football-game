# 6.1 Squads

**Status:** Outline

Players who differ: every player carries their own ratings, and a team is a squad with players assigned to its formation's cells. Until now every attribute has been exercised through live stat tuning on otherwise identical players. Picking the team and changing it is [6.2 Team Management](6.2-team-management.md).

**Notes:**

- **Every attribute already works; this judges them together.** Each was wired in with its mechanic and checked against its design doc there — see those milestones' Attributes fields. What no single milestone could show is whether the ratings combine into players who feel different: does who you control change how you play?
- **Watch for speed swamping everything.** It's SWOS's known balance fault — see [Weaknesses](../../sources/swos/overview.md#weaknesses). Log it, don't fix it here: the clone matches the reference, and fixing it is original design
- **Squad data is synthetic** — hand-authored teams, never real squads, per [Licensing and IP](../../docs/decisions/licensing-and-ip.md#real-football)
- **Kit clashes are resolved here**, per [Kits](../../docs/design/match-engine/kits.md). Until now every match was two fixed test teams whose kits were picked not to clash; authored teams are the first that can meet in kits too alike. Each team carries a primary and a secondary kit from here
- **Squads are bigger than eleven**, so [6.2](6.2-team-management.md) has a bench to bring players on from. Nobody off the pitch does anything yet
- **A player's natural position is recorded but does nothing.** Out-of-position effects wait for [6.2](6.2-team-management.md), the first time a player can be put in a cell that isn't theirs

**Attributes:** all of them — the seven outfield ratings and the keeper's qualities — now set per player rather than per session.

**Testing:** kit clash resolution, exact against the reference's tables per [Kits](../../docs/design/match-engine/kits.md)'s acceptance criteria. Rating bounds — every authored player sits within the reference's ranges. The simulation is tuned against those bounds, and a value outside them misbehaves rather than just being strong, per [Career Mode Mechanics](../../sources/swos/career-mode-mechanics.md#database-modding-stayed-within-the-original-bounds).
