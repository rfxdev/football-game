# 2.3 Contests

**Status:** Outline

1v1 against an outfield opponent, contesting the ball every way the reference allows: slide tackles and passive duels on a carrier, the same on a loose ball, contested headers, and foul **detection**. The act of contesting, not the choice — the drill scripts what the opponent does; when the AI slides or heads is [3.3 Decision Loop](3.3-decision-loop.md)'s. The foul stops play; the free kick that follows is [5.3 Free Kicks and Penalties](5.3-free-kicks-and-penalties.md). The mechanism is [The Passive Duel](../../docs/design/match-engine/on-the-ball-mechanics.md#the-passive-duel), [Fouls](../../docs/design/match-engine/on-the-ball-mechanics.md#fouls) and [Same-Tick Contacts](../../docs/design/match-engine/on-the-ball-mechanics.md#same-tick-contacts).

**Notes:**

- **Two ways to lose the ball, and only one of them involves the button.** The passive duel is easy to overlook because it has no input attached, and it is doing a lot of the work
- **A loose ball is contested too, not only a carried one.** Two players sliding for one loose ball is where a same-tick contact is likeliest
- **Contested headers are the aerial half, and the drill scripts them.** A high ball comes in and the opponent is set to jump for it. There is no contest mechanism to build — the first header to connect wins
- **Same-tick contacts are ours, not the reference's.** Too rare to wait for in play — force each in the drill
- **The foul rule turns on whether the ball was touched first, not on the angle.** A trailing chase rarely wins the ball cleanly, so it lands in the from-behind check — worth understanding before tuning it, because the lever is not where it looks
- **Ball control's pressure rule gets judged here**, built at [1.1](1.1-movement-and-possession.md): too many direction changes with a defender near loses touch
- **The first opposing outfield player, so the first time two outfield kits share the screen.** Nothing new is built, but it is the first real test of the [Kits](../../docs/design/match-engine/kits.md) criterion that a player's side reads at a glance — a kit legible on its own can blur against another mid-tackle
- Cards and injuries roll off this detection but are staged in [5.4 Cards and Injuries](5.4-cards-and-injuries.md)

**Attributes:** Tackling and Ball Control together decide the passive duel; Tackling settles two slides on the same tick, and Heading two headers. Values in [On-the-Ball Mechanics](../../docs/design/match-engine/on-the-ball-mechanics.md). Tackling's other effect, recovery after a slide, arrives earlier with the slide itself in [1.3](1.3-contextual-action.md).

**Sprites:** a player brought down by a foul.
