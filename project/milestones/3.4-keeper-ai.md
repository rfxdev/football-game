# 3.4 Keeper AI

**Status:** Outline

The keeper stops being scaffolding and becomes a real player: holds its line, comes for what it should come for, and stays where it should stay.

**Notes:**

- **Its own milestone because its failure is unmissable.** Every other player can be slightly wrong without anyone noticing; a keeper drifting upfield with the play cannot. That asymmetry is why it gets separated from [3.3 Decision Loop](3.3-decision-loop.md) rather than folded into it
- Replaces the line-holding placeholder from [2.1 Keeper Drill](2.1-keeper-drill.md). Shooting was tuned against that placeholder, so expect shooting to need re-judging once the keeper is real — that is the milestone working, not a regression
- Deliberately a special case, with rules of its own and no anchor, per [The Keeper](../../docs/design/match-engine/team-ai-and-decision-making.md#the-keeper). Its dive and save rules aren't researched yet — see that doc's Open Questions

**Attributes:** keeper qualities — positioning, speed of vision, and diving speed and range. The reference doesn't store these as separate ratings; all of them improve with the keeper's value, per [Player Ratings](../../sources/swos/player-ratings.md). Finishing has fed the save chance since [2.1](2.1-keeper-drill.md); what arrives here is the keeper's own side of that calculation, which the placeholder never had.

**Sprites:** the keeper's full repertoire — dives, catches, and holding and releasing the ball.
