# 2.1 Keeper Drill

**Status:** Outline

1v1 against a deliberately simple keeper that holds its line. The first opposition of any kind, and the first time a goal is worth scoring. Adds a goalmouth — posts and a net — but no pitch and no markings.

**Notes:**

- **A goalmouth and a penalty box, not a pitch.** The goal *predicate* already exists from [0.2 Sandbox Scene](0.2-sandbox-scene.md); what this adds is something visible to aim at, placed on an arbitrary line in a still-fixed frame. The box comes with it because Finishing needs one to test against, per the Attributes field below. The real goal line, both goals and the pitch around them arrive at [3.0 Pitch and Camera](3.0-pitch-and-camera.md), which is the milestone that repositions this one rather than rebuilding it

- **The keeper here is scaffolding, not the real thing.** It exists so there is something to shoot past. It becomes a real player at [3.4 Keeper AI](3.4-keeper-ai.md), and treating it as finished before then will hide shooting problems behind keeper problems
- **A drill, not a match.** The ball going out resets the scenario, per [0.2 Sandbox Scene](0.2-sandbox-scene.md). Nothing here needs the restart system
- **First appearance of the fairness problem.** You act on direct input; the keeper acts on a perception loop. Note anything that felt *psychic* or *asleep* — that axis stays live for the rest of the project, per [Team AI and Decision Making](../../docs/design/match-engine/team-ai-and-decision-making.md)
- **Kits arrive here, because the keeper is the first sprite not wearing your kit**, built to [Kits](../../docs/design/match-engine/kits.md). Its recolouring by palette swap means every placeholder from 1.1–1.3 needs its kit regions in the reserved colours by now — cheapest if they were drawn that way from the start

**Attributes:** Finishing, deferred from [1.2 Kicking](1.2-kicking.md) because it changes a shot's outcome rather than the kick. It modifies accuracy and adds a power multiplier inside the box only — never on headers or from outside it — and the result feeds the keeper's save chance. Shot Power gets its first real judgement here too, against something trying to stop it.

**Sprites:** the keeper, in a kit that isn't yours, the goalmouth — posts, bar, and the layered net from [Pitch and Environment](../../docs/design/match-engine/pitch-and-environment.md) — and the penalty box marking around it.

**Testing:** invariants start here, as a shared per-tick check that rides along with every test after — exactly zero or one player in possession, no NaN or over-speed positions, ball in the scenario or the scenario reset, score only ever rising.
