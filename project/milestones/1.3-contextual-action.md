# 1.3 Contextual Action

**Status:** Outline

The action button branches on the ball's height: low it slides, high it heads. Both actions exist here; contesting them with anyone waits for [2.3 Contests](2.3-contests.md). The mechanism is [The Action Button](../../docs/design/match-engine/on-the-ball-mechanics.md#the-action-button), [Sliding](../../docs/design/match-engine/on-the-ball-mechanics.md#sliding) and [Heading](../../docs/design/match-engine/on-the-ball-mechanics.md#heading).

**Notes:**

- **One milestone, not two.** Slide and header share the button, and the ball's height at the press decides which fires. They are one branch — neither can be built without deciding the other, and splitting them would mean building the branch twice
- **The slide is a committed action, not a fast run, and it should feel like one.** The lunge is the same for every player and faster than anyone runs
- **Slide strength is pure input, so it's here rather than waiting for an opponent.** It shows twice — in how hard the ball comes off the connect and in how long the slider lies down after
- **The two headers aim differently, both at contact.** Worth confirming both exist before judging either
- Fouls are not here. A slide that misses the ball and hits a player is [2.3 Contests](2.3-contests.md); with nobody to hit, this milestone covers the lunge, the connect and the recovery

**Attributes:** Tackling sets the recovery after a strong slide; Heading sets how hard a header is struck. The lunge, and a header's reach and aim, read none. Values in [On-the-Ball Mechanics](../../docs/design/match-engine/on-the-ball-mechanics.md).

**Sprites:** the slide and the lying-down recovery after it, and both headers. The slide's frames fit its lunge rather than setting it. The jump is the first time a player leaves the ground, so it takes the shadow and sort-order conventions the ball already uses, per [Ball Physics](../../docs/design/match-engine/ball-physics-and-aerial-simulation.md#showing-height).
