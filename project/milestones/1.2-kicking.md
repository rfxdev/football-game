# 1.2 Kicking

**Status:** Outline

Tap for a pass, hold for a shot or lob, with aftertouch on the ball in flight. Still one player and no opposition — this is the kick itself, not what anyone does about it. The mechanism is [Kicking](../../docs/design/match-engine/on-the-ball-mechanics.md#kicking).

**Notes:**

- **Relative aftertouch is the thing to get right.** Getting it wrong is subtle and pervasive — it reads as "the ball does the wrong thing sometimes" rather than as a specific bug
- **Aftertouch has a timing dimension as well as a direction one**, since it fades the longer you wait. Both need to be live before this can be judged
- Lofted kicking is the first *mechanic* built on the fake-Z foundation from [0.2 Sandbox Scene](0.2-sandbox-scene.md). Heading, the other one, waits for [1.3 Contextual Action](1.3-contextual-action.md)
- Dead-ball kicks in Phase 5 reuse this control scheme unchanged, so what lands here is what they inherit

**Attributes:** Passing, Shot Power and Finishing, per [Kicking](../../docs/design/match-engine/on-the-ball-mechanics.md#kicking). Shot Power is judged here. Passing's cone and length can't be until [2.2](2.2-two-attackers.md) gives a pass somewhere to go. Finishing waits for [2.1](2.1-keeper-drill.md) — it changes a shot's outcome, not the kick, and there is nothing here to beat.

**Sprites:** the kick.

**Testing:** tap-versus-hold classifies correctly right at the duration boundary, since a frame's error either side is a shot the player didn't ask for.
