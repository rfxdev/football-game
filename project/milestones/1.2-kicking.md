# 1.2 Kicking

**Status:** Outline

Tap for a pass, hold for a shot or lob, with aftertouch on the ball in flight. Still one player and no opposition — this is the kick itself, not what anyone does about it.

**Notes:**

- **Aftertouch is relative to the kick's own direction, not absolute up/down on the stick.** Holding with the kick drives it low, pulling against it sends it high, centring lobs it, angling curls it. Getting this wrong is subtle and pervasive — it will read as "the ball does the wrong thing sometimes" rather than as a specific bug
- **Aftertouch fades the longer you wait.** The sooner the stick moves after the kick, the more dramatic the bend — so the mechanic has a timing dimension as well as a direction one, and both need to be live before this can be judged
- Lofted kicking is the first *mechanic* built on the fake-Z foundation from [0.2 Sandbox Scene](0.2-sandbox-scene.md). Heading, the other one, waits for [1.3 Contextual Action](1.3-contextual-action.md)
- Dead-ball kicks in Phase 5 reuse this control scheme unchanged — there is no separate input method for set pieces, so what lands here is what they inherit

**Attributes:** Passing widens the maximum pass length and the cone within which a teammate counts as a target — the cone can't be judged until [2.2](2.2-two-attackers.md) gives a pass somewhere to go. Shot Power sets a shot's raw power and distance. Finishing waits for [2.1](2.1-keeper-drill.md) — it changes a shot's accuracy and the keeper's chance of saving it, not the kick itself, and there is nothing here to beat.

**Sprites:** the kick.

**Testing:** tap-versus-hold classifies correctly right at the duration boundary, since a frame's error either side is a shot the player didn't ask for.
