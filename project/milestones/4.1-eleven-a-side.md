# 4.1 Eleven a Side

**Status:** Outline

Full-size pitch, eleven players a side, a 4-4-2 on the 5×5 formation grid. Scaling the Phase 3 machinery rather than replacing it.

**Notes:**

- **This is scaling, not re-architecture.** Five-a-side is the first rung of the same machinery, per [Formation and Shape System](../../docs/design/match-engine/formation-and-shape-system.md). The 2×2 grid becoming 5×5 is mostly data, plus code for what the extra cells add. **If this turns into a rewrite, the problem is in [3.2](3.2-shape-and-home-zones.md)'s design and that is where to fix it** — pushing through here would mean maintaining two shape systems
- **Central columns and more rows are what's new.** The wide-column rules carry over from [3.2](3.2-shape-and-home-zones.md) unchanged; central cells follow the block with no adjustment of their own
- Doing the hard AI work at five and scaling to eleven is deliberate: blobbing is legible at five and soup at eleven
- Still no restarts. Eleven a side that resets on out-of-play is perfectly playable, just arcade-ish

**360 Testing:** [3.2](3.2-shape-and-home-zones.md)'s shape-recovery test and the team-spread floor both need widening — they were written against five players on a small pitch, and neither the radii nor the spread figures survive the change of scale unexamined.
