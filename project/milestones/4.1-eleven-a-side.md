# 4.1 Eleven a Side

**Status:** Outline

Eleven players a side on [3.0](3.0-pitch-and-camera.md)'s pitch, a 4-4-2 on the 5×5 formation grid. Scaling the Phase 3 machinery rather than replacing it — the pitch and the grid don't change, only the layout and who is on it.

**Notes:**

- **This is scaling, not re-architecture.** Five-a-side is the first rung of the same machinery, per [Formation and Shape System](../../docs/design/match-engine/formation-and-shape-system.md): the same 5×5 grid, so what's new is a layout and six more players. **If this needs code beyond that, the problem is in [3.2](3.2-shape-and-home-zones.md)'s design and that is where to fix it** — pushing through here would mean maintaining two shape systems
- **Full rows and the wide columns are what's new** — several players sharing a row, and full-backs and wide midfielders, so the wide-column rules [3.2](3.2-shape-and-home-zones.md) built are first judged in play here. Every other cell follows the block with no adjustment of its own, as [3.2](3.2-shape-and-home-zones.md)'s already do
- Doing the hard AI work at five and scaling to eleven is deliberate: blobbing is legible at five and soup at eleven
- Still no restarts — out of play resets the scenario until Phase 5, per [Roadmap](../roadmap.md#notes-on-the-order)

**360 Testing:** [3.2](3.2-shape-and-home-zones.md)'s shape-recovery test and the team-spread floor both need widening — they were written against five players with the whole pitch to spread across, and neither the radii nor the spread figures survive eleven unexamined.
