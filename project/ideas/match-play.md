# Match Play

- **Variety without a second button** — attacking play that isn't all direct, carried by context and stick position, since one button is a [pillar](../../docs/design/game-vision-and-design-goals.md#design-pillars). What SWOS's single button costs is in [One Action Button](../../sources/swos/overview.md#one-action-button)
- **Injury time** — the clock keeps running through celebrations, injuries and other stoppages, and the time lost is added on at the end of each half. A long stoppage or a late goal carries the match past 90 with something still at stake, rather than the clock quietly waiting. Pausing and the tactics screen still stop it
