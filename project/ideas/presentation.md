# Presentation

## Two Directions

- **2D at high resolution** — the same top-down game, drawn in far more detail than the reference's pixels allow: kits, faces, animation that reads at a glance. Scaled freely, so every display shows the same view
- **3D** — a camera free to move: replays from any angle, a broadcast view, a close-up on a goal. Light and weather across the whole scene, and animation that blends rather than steps between frames

## Additions

- **Zoom on the right stick** — out to the whole width of the pitch, as the Xbox version does, to see the whole of play when you want it
- **Pitch wear** — slide marks that stay for the rest of the match, as the Xbox version has, so the pitch shows the match it has had
- **UI overhaul** — squad comparison, transfer browsing, the tactics preview. Most of what makes SWOS's manager side hard to use is the UI
